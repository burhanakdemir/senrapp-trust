## What SENRAPP claims (and does not)

**Claims (factual):**

- Private chats use end-to-end encryption; the operator does not keep readable message bodies as a server chat archive.
- Message history is primarily device-local.
- The phone address book is not uploaded as a contact list.
- Official Android and iOS builds are distributed via Google Play and the App Store.

**Does not claim:**

- Absolute / “unhackable” security  
- Military-grade marketing slogans  
- Completed trademark registration until the certificate is published on the About page  
- That SENRAPP is a bank, emergency service (112), or party to marketplace sales
